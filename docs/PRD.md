# Enterprise Logistics Management System

## Purpose and boundaries

ELMS manages the distribution of educational materials for IMS Learning Resources. Employees work from one payment-aware queue rather than coordinating spreadsheets. Admissions owns student identity, enrolment and address; Finance owns fees and milestones. There is no student login, payment collection, refund execution, HR or academic scheduling.

## Users and permissions

Super Admin manages employees, configuration and every operational module. Logistics Manager manages dispatches, transfers, kit definitions, courier rules, templates, imports and reports, but cannot manage employees. Dispatch Executive reads students and milestones, creates and tracks dispatches and uploads operational data. Warehouse Staff works with inventory and a limited packing station: identifiers and material quantities only, with no student contacts, payments or courier handover. Viewer/Auditor reads operational records and exports reports; bulk student PII export is reserved for Manager and Super Admin. Every employee can maintain their profile and read permitted internal conversations; auditors cannot create tasks. Data providers authenticate separately using scoped integration keys and cannot use employee screens.

## Core journeys

1. Administrator bootstraps one employee using environment credentials, then configures centres, course prices, items, kits, courier rules and serviceable postcodes.
2. Admissions sends enrolments; Finance sends milestones. Cleared kits enter the dispatch queue. Executive checks exceptions, selects records and packs. Stock and ledger commit together.
3. Executive enters AWB, hands over and records tracking events. Outbox messages follow transitions. Returned parcels follow one-time condition reconciliation.
4. Manager initiates a transfer, reviews monetary snapshot and approves a resolution. Data providers confirm the new enrolment/milestones before new material unlocks.
5. Warehouse staff receive vendor goods, move goods between centres and review low-stock demand. Manager exports requisitions and reports.
6. User resets an expired credential through email, changes password and revokes devices. Administrator disables an employee without deleting history.

## Architecture decision

The user approved Next.js and Fastify with TypeScript during implementation. Use Next.js App Router for the employee application, Fastify for typed API routes and Prisma with PostgreSQL for persistence. Business logic and transactional data access remain independent of HTTP framework. Keep a single API process with a durable notification outbox; avoid microservices.

## Data and business rules

Students relate to course, centre, payment plan and milestones. Kits relate to course and BOM items. Dispatches preserve enrolment version, warehouse and packed material cost/quantity snapshots. Inventory and ledger relate to centre and item. Transfers preserve financial snapshots and approval identity. Centre orders and requisitions have line records. Notifications, imports and audit records preserve outcomes. Unique constraints prevent duplicate dispatch, AWB, stock balance, return receipt and repeated provider events.

BR-01 through BR-10 are mandatory: cleared milestones; kit-specific partial eligibility; automatic hold/drop blocks; immediate transfer holds; deduction of already shipped materials; reasoned courier override; serviceability before dispatch; employee audit; upstream data read-only; restricted notification templates. Money uses decimal storage and integer-paise calculations. Timestamps are UTC; presentation uses Asia/Kolkata and INR.

## Non-functional requirements

Target dashboard response under 3 seconds and 500-record bulk planning under 10 seconds; validate with representative 50,000-student data before accepting capacity. Target 99.5% availability requires monitored hosting, not a code-only guarantee. Paginated queries, relational indexes and bounded exports limit load. WCAG 2.2 AA is the accessibility target: semantics, labels, focus, keyboard dialogs, contrast and reduced motion. Support latest two Chrome, Firefox and Edge releases, tablet and mobile widths.

Security includes scoped ingestion credentials, least privilege, session revocation, rate limits, CSRF origin checks, safe multipart bounds, SQL parameterisation, output encoding, safe downloads and redacted logs. Production requires HTTPS, encrypted managed storage, restricted DB role and secret rotation. Audit rows cannot be updated/deleted by the application role. Backups run daily with one-year retention and restore drills. PII retention defaults to three years after completion; operational identifiers and audit metadata remain without addresses.

## Integrations and unresolved inputs

No ERP endpoint contracts, messaging account, SMTP account, authoritative postcode feed, regional fee policy or hosting account was supplied. Provide scoped push APIs and reviewed imports, manual courier tracking (API automation is explicitly future scope), configurable real provider adapters and deployment documentation. No invented financial or operational master data is loaded. Management must supply price/milestone policy, validated courier serviceability and notification registration/templates. Scanned PDFs require an external OCR review process.

## Success and release acceptance

Targets: zero unauthorised dispatches; planning under 30 minutes; tracking enquiries reduced 40%; RTO cost reduced 15%. Baselines and business measurements are needed after rollout. Run the documented two-week shadow period against Excel before cutover. Release requires builds, lint, critical transaction/permission/integration tests, accessible browser workflow verification, migration rehearsal, backup restore verification, configured provider testing and load testing. External operational dependencies must be stated honestly rather than simulated.
